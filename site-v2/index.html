04 / What we learned

Evidence over hype

Our strongest result isn't the number of tasks. It's the controlled test of whether the model uses the measured signals.

Time order matters

Temporal QA drops from 80.3% on real series to 46.5% after time shuffling.

Signal identity matters

Workload classification drops from 88.7% to 37.8% when another kernel's series replaces the original while text summaries remain fixed.

We report the limits

Specialized baselines outperform the model on individual tasks. The data come from one H100, with rule-derived diagnosis references and one multi-task training seed.
